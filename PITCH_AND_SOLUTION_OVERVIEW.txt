[2-MINUTE EXECUTIVE BRIEF]
The Problem:
When regulatory bodies (such as the OCC, CFPB, SEC, FINRA, or Federal Reserve) 
issue a consent order or inquiry requiring population remediation (e.g., fee 
refunds, rate discrepancies, misallocated charges), enterprise institutions must 
take millions of customer records and filter them down to the exact in-scope cohort.
Today, this process is broken:
- First-Line Compliance writes ambiguous business requirements in emails/Word docs.
- Data analysts interpret those rules and run isolated SQL scripts.
- The population counts mismatch, logic is lost in translation, and iterations take 
  4 to 8 weeks per scoping cycle.
- When regulators or internal audit ask for mathematical proof of why accounts were 
  excluded, institutions face multi-million dollar fines or failed audits due to 
  lack of line-by-line lineage.

The Solution:
Our Traceable Waterfall Scoping & Governance Platform creates a closed-loop, dual-role 
operating environment:
1. First-Line Risk & Compliance (FRC) formulates structured waterfall steps 
   (Starting Population, Exclusions, Deductions) with business justifications and 
   target timelines.
2. Quantitative Analysts execute verified analytics, certifying in-scope/excluded 
   counts, linking SQL query artifacts, and validating baseline integrity.
3. The system enforces deterministic governance: each step dynamically recalculates 
   the downstream population funnel, tracks net working days saved, logs an 
   immutable chronological audit trail, and automatically alerts all stakeholders.
4. Leadership gains single-pane visibility through real-time population trendlines 
   and one-click audit certification packs.
